WoundWise LLC Services

Professional Wound Care Designed Around Your Recovery

WoundWise LLC provides specialized in-home wound care services throughout New York City and Long Island. Our care is designed to promote safe healing, prevent complications, and support patients in the comfort of home.

01

Traumatic Wounds

Care for wounds caused by injuries, accidents, cuts, burns, surgical openings, or trauma-related complications. We focus on infection prevention, proper dressing, and safe healing.

Request Care →
02

Pressure Related Wounds

Treatment and monitoring for pressure injuries, bed sores, and wounds caused by prolonged pressure, especially in patients with limited mobility.

Request Care →
03

Circulation Related Wounds

Support for wounds related to poor blood flow, diabetic ulcers, venous ulcers, arterial wounds, and chronic healing challenges.

Request Care →
04

Specialized Wound Care

Advanced care for complex, chronic, or slow-healing wounds requiring personalized treatment plans, regular follow-up, and professional wound management.
