About WoundWise LLC

Expert Wound Care Delivered To Your Home

WoundWise LLC provides compassionate, professional, and personalized wound care services in the comfort of your home. We help patients throughout New York City and Long Island recover safely with treatment plans designed around their needs.

Our approach focuses on comfort, dignity, clinical excellence, and consistent follow-up. We work closely with patients, families, caregivers, and healthcare providers to support faster healing and better quality of life.

Meet Your Provider

Bhupinderjit Singh, NP

Founder & Nurse Practitioner

Bhupinderjit Singh is a dedicated Nurse Practitioner with over 10 years of clinical experience across inpatient, outpatient, urgent care, long-term care, and home-based healthcare settings.

He has served patients throughout New York with a strong focus on evidence-based treatment, compassionate care, and improved healing outcomes. His experience includes work at NewYork-Presbyterian Hospital as well as teaching and mentoring future healthcare professionals as a Clinical Adjunct Professor.

Holding both a Master of Science in Nursing and a Master of Business Administration, Bhupinderjit combines clinical expertise with leadership and operational excellence to deliver exceptional patient-centered care.
